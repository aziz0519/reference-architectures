# AI Architect Interview Preparation

## Architecture Solution Walkthrough
* **Business requirements:** show the business needs and expected outcomes
* **Options & recommendation:** compare solutions and explain your choice
* **Authorized information access:** show employees retrieving only permitted information
* **Answer quality:** evaluate accuracy and relevance
* **Operational costs:** explain how ongoing spending will be managed
* **System monitoring:** track system health and performance
* **Preliminary business case:** explain how to assess whether the investment makes financial sense


## Interview Focus
* Establish rapport quickly
    * Build trust with leaders, stakeholders and technical teams.
    * Be professional, enthusiatic and approachable
    * Listen carefully and ask thoughtful questions
    * Show interest in the organization and its challenges
